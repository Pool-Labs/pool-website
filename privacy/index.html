Privacy policy

Last updated 21 November 2026

Pool is made by Pool Labs Inc. (“Pool”, “we”, “us”). This policy covers this website, poolapp.co, and the Pool early access list. The Pool app isn’t open yet. It will come with its own privacy policy, which you’ll see before you open an account.

What we collect

  • Your email address, when you join the early access list.
  • Your invite details: the invite code we make for you, the code of the friend who invited you (if someone did), and how many friends have joined with your link.
  • Basic technical data that our hosting provider records whenever anyone loads a website, such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested.
  • An invite code on your device. If you arrive through a friend’s invite link, your browser keeps their code in local storage until you join, so your friend gets the credit. It is deleted once you join, and it never leaves your device unless you sign up.

We don’t use analytics, advertising or tracking cookies on this website.

How we use it

  • To add you to the early access list and email you about Pool, including your invite when Pool launches.
  • To count your invites, which we use to order the early access list and to set your droplet multiplier when Pool launches.
  • To keep the list free of spam and abuse.

We don’t sell your personal information, and we don’t share it for advertising.

Who helps us

  • Loops (loops.so) stores the early access list and sends our emails.
  • Vercel (vercel.com) hosts this website and runs the sign-up.

They handle your information only to provide these services to us. We may also share information when the law requires it, or with a company that takes over Pool, which would have to keep following this policy.

Your choices

  • Every email we send has an unsubscribe link.
  • You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing privacy@poolapp.co. We reply within 30 days.
  • Depending on where you live, for example California, the EU or the UK, you may have further privacy rights. We honour these requests for everyone, wherever you live.

How long we keep it

We keep your early access details until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete them. If you open a Pool account, the app’s privacy policy covers that account.

Security

This website uses encrypted connections, and only the people who run Pool can see the early access list. No system is perfectly secure, but we work to protect your information and will tell you if something goes wrong that affects it.

Children

This website isn’t meant for children under 13, and we don’t knowingly collect their information. If you think a child has joined the list, email us and we’ll delete it.

Where your information is stored

Pool is based in the United States, and our providers store information in the United States.

Changes

If we change this policy, we’ll update the date at the top. For changes that matter, we’ll also email the early access list.
